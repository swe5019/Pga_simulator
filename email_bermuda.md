Hi,

Ownership projections for the Butterfield Bermuda Championship are up on
SlateSims. The full 120 golfer field is loaded with salaries, win and top ten
equity, market make cut prices, and a leverage read on every name.

The ownership model is the same one that has been running all season, trained
on 32 completed events, and its average error on a held out event is 2.5
percentage points. Worth knowing what that means in practice: on a golfer
projected at 20% ownership, being out by two and a half points either way is
normal and you should not build around small differences.

A few things to look at this week:

Benjamin James is the clear chalk at 35.6%, which is a big number for a field
this flat. The whole board is priced tight, so nearly every golfer grades out
as chalk relative to his odds, and that makes the few genuinely contrarian
plays more valuable than usual.

Ownership totals 600% across the field, which is exactly one full six man
lineup, so the numbers reconcile against each other rather than being a list
of independent guesses.

Make cut prices are in as well, so the cut column is the market number rather
than my own guess, and the simulator is calibrated to it. 118 of the 120
golfers are priced. The two who are not show a 50% placeholder and are marked
in the table so you can see at a glance that the number is a stand in.
