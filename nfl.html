NFL Advanced Stats

Usage and efficiency, built from nflverse play by play, snap counts and Next Gen Stats. DFS is won on opportunity (snap share, target share, red zone looks) far more than on efficiency, so those lead. Edge is the one to read first: it compares a player's usage rank against his salary rank, so a positive number means the market has not caught up to his role yet. Every rate is recomputed over the week range you pick, not averaged across weeks, so a role that changed in week 3 shows up cleanly.
